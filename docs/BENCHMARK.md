# Live research and draft acceptance

The 50-candidate research run started on 10 October 2026. It researches official public websites only, with full Chromium rendering, a 30-attempt budget, depth 3 and two independent company workers. It never imports prospects, probes recipient servers or sends email. The sample contains 25 US and 25 UK discovery hints, 26 Campaign A candidates and 24 Campaign B candidates. Architecture/design services and skincare/beauty stores are represented; this does not establish quality across every industry.

The original run finished all 50 attempts in 5,207.55 seconds: 29 partial site reports and 21 failed attempts, with 774 captured/rendered pages and 769 distinct final URLs across companies. Twenty consecutive late attempts failed with OS DNS errors; four exact failed hosts subsequently resolved in read-only checks. Failed attempts are not usable prospects. [The dated result snapshot](benchmark-progress.json) preserves all outcomes. Private streamed evidence and `summary.json` are under `data/benchmarks/run-20261010-074901-UTC-07b57d`. Country hints, company labels and public email counts are discovery inputs, not approved buyer matches or verified recipients.

## Findings applied to the implementation

- Redirected URLs could capture the same final page twice. Final URLs are now deduplicated; canonical/redirect aliases do not inflate the attempt counter. Remaining coverage excludes already-seen aliases. A regression test exercises redirected www aliases, canonical URLs and exhausted budgets.
- The longer run exposed a browser callback race on a store's video requests. Swallowing a failed terminal abort/fulfill could leave Playwright waiting forever; cleanup cancellation then produced orphan callback warnings. A failed fulfill now attempts one terminal abort, preserving the DOM when that settles the request. If abort also fails, routing stops and the affected page closes, then the failure propagates through handlers marked to consume errors. An unowned abort failure closes its browser context. No direct-network fallback is used. Real Chromium fixtures exercise both recovery/close branches and unowned failure. A fresh live check of the affected product page retained rendered evidence without callback warnings, while reporting resource/robots/POST/script limitations.
- Several official sites publish another-domain business inbox. These were previously discarded. The corrected extractor preserves them as unapproved source/hash-linked candidates, with operator ownership review required before importing or probing. Stale source evidence blocks verification and SMTP; shared-company inbox conflicts cannot silently attach the wrong lead. A fresh one-page Harvest contact check retained one candidate, zero automatic contacts and no callback warning in 25.54 seconds (`crawler-alias-contact-check.json`). This is source extraction evidence, not proof of inbox ownership or validity. The original 50-company process predates this correction too.
- The 50-company process started before these corrections and retained its original loaded implementation. Its raw reports are observational evidence and may include repeated final URLs and callback warnings. They are not a clean acceptance run of the corrected code. Its `review-context.json` preserves this boundary; the progress snapshot reports unique URLs separately. Future runs record source-file and dependency-lock hashes plus Python/Playwright runtime versions at startup.

## Draft review

Historical company-level previews use `draft_sequence`, an offline template renderer. The operational draft route now uses `email_writer.generate_sequence` and requires a configured AI provider; these historical samples do not validate that model or writer. `scripts/draft_review.py` requires a completed company report, a supported single offer, a saved official-page observation quote, a saved country quote and an official company name. WhatsApp previews require explicit channel-fit and permission unknowns. These checks protect provenance; they cannot prove that an interpretation or suggestion is correct.

Twenty distinct real company sequences have received source/copy review: **80 messages, not 80 prospects**, with five companies in each A-US, A-UK, B-US and B-UK group. All remain unapproved company-level previews. Buyer identity, business eligibility, mailbox validity and sender configuration are unconfirmed; no contact is manufactured to satisfy a draft count. The private review is `data/draft-reviews/pass-012/draft-review.html`. The 20-company source/copy review is complete; independent operator/buyer/recipient approval and live pilot acceptance remain pending. Actual Chromium opened all 60 follow-up disclosures at a 390 px viewport, with no horizontal overflow, remote requests or JavaScript errors. First messages contain 79–95 words. The limited structural/claim-pattern checks are not a guarantee of semantic accuracy; `draft-review-check.json` records the boundary.

The annotations distinguish an observed legacy contact-page placeholder from unknown traffic or business impact, respect a firm's selective commission capacity, acknowledge existing inquiry routes and avoid invented conversion losses, case studies or delivered assets. A seasonal-store WhatsApp idea explicitly depends on customer preference, valid opt-in, message requirements and integration feasibility. Published store claims about skincare efficacy are not endorsed or used as medical advice. Existing FAQs, chat widgets, contact routes and stated response times are acknowledged. One design candidate offers visualisation/film services, so its brief/file-transfer FAQ idea reflects that service rather than pretending it is a conventional architecture firm. The Goldstein Heather preview uses preserved evidence from a timed-out partial report; no preview establishes a complete site audit.


Do not restart an active run merely because a UI/tool wait ends. Use its saved manifest and original process state to distinguish a running crawl from a failed one; the original 50-company run is now terminal. Inspect partial coverage and source context before accepting a finding. The preview tool reads saved files only; it does not use a database or load provider/sender credentials. Outputs stay private and are excluded from the source release.

Before live outreach, independently check geography, company/buyer relevance, contact ownership, fresh conservative Reacher results, all four touches and the applicable outreach basis. Actual VPS/container memory, mailbox/domain/HTTPS and controlled-recipient acceptance remain deferred until the user's infrastructure is configured. Workstation timings are not a 4 GB VPS guarantee.
