# Page-specific readability and focused drafts

The visual/journey audit now samples solid text/background contrast in two DOM
reads, alongside form labels, overflow and public enquiry links. This interprets
computed page styling, not screenshot pixels or subjective visual taste. The
reference is W3C SC 1.4.3: normal text 4.5:1 and large text 3:1
(https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html).

The reader requires loaded styles, visible paragraph/heading text, opaque solid
colors and consistent font/color evidence. It skips images, gradients, transparency,
filters, blending, text shadows and ambiguous pseudo-element backgrounds. The
consumer independently recomputes contrast from RGB/font data before allowing a
finding. One representative affected block per viewport includes text, selector,
colors, font size, screenshot and repeated-sample proof. This is not a full WCAG
conformance audit or proof of lost sales.

Confirmed readability findings outrank general lab performance findings in the
email brief. The proposed fix describes the affected text/background treatment,
retains brand intent and requires a repeat check. Other lab evidence stays saved
in research/provenance. Optional calling/chatbot services remain available but are
not mandatory additions to an unrelated website finding.

A homepage brand can replace a search-result headline in the email greeting only
when a published title segment exactly matches the domain's brand label and is
also present in saved homepage text. The stored company identity is unchanged;
foreign-source titles and unsupported names fall back to that identity.
