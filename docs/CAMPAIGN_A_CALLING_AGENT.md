# Campaign A: AI calling agent

Campaign A now supports four primary services: website/new service page,
website improvement/redesign, chatbot with human handoff, and AI calling agent.
One prospect receives one primary offer, selected from current business evidence.
This change implements discovery, qualification, an offer plan, a call-flow proof
preview and AI email drafting. It does not install a telephony service, place calls
or connect a client's calendar/CRM.

## Evidence and selection

The saved source must positively connect a phone/callback route to a relevant task:

| Published process | Proposed use case | Concrete help | Intended benefit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Call for a quote or discuss requirements | Lead qualification | Ask service, location and timeline; send an agreed summary and route to the team | Give the team useful enquiry details before follow-up |
| Call to book or arrange an appointment | Booking assistance | Capture preferences; connect an approved calendar; check availability, confirm with caller, hand off when needed | Reduce manual scheduling steps and produce complete appointment requests |
| Customer support telephone route | Customer support | Answer from approved knowledge; transfer unresolved/sensitive requests with context | Consistent routine answers and useful context for human support |

A bare phone number, generic Get a Quote button or a low Lighthouse score is
insufficient. Recruitment/emergency lines, negative statements, unrelated domains
and Campaign B are excluded. Existing phone tools are not assumed absent.
Mode is derived from the exact source quote, with no extra qualification form.
Real call volume, missed calls and customer demand remain unknown until confirmed.

## Pitch plan

1. Name the published business process in one short sentence.
2. Offer one concrete call flow with two relevant handling steps.
3. Explain its practical benefit as an intended goal, not a claimed present failure.
4. Offer a short tailored example/walkthrough as the next step.

Example direction for a business that publishes phone quote enquiries: an AI
calling agent could ask about the requested service, location and timeline, then
pass a useful summary to the team so they can assess fit before following up.
The writer composes the email individually; this example is not a fixed template.

Do not claim lost leads, missed calls, overwhelmed staff, high volume, guaranteed
bookings, existing integrations or completed working demos without evidence.
Do not use PageSpeed findings to justify a phone-agent offer. Lab diagnostics stay
in provenance but are omitted from the calling writer's evidence packet and pitch.
The call-flow preview is local, source-backed and unsent, not a deployed agent.

## Implementation plan after client agreement

- Confirm one inbound or customer-requested callback process, business owner and scope.
- Agree approved questions/answers, qualification criteria, human transfer and failure handling.
- Configure the agreed telephony provider; calendar/CRM access needs explicit integration work.
- Review caller disclosure and applicable callback/recording requirements before activation.
- Test synthetic conversations, unavailable calendar/CRM and failed transfers before a bounded pilot.
- Compare summary accuracy, booking correctness and handoff quality; expand only after review.

The outreach software does not dial prospects or enable a calling campaign as part
of selecting this service. Existing global email controls and final review remain.
