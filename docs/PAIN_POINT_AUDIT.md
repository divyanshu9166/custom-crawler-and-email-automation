The current Spotless email proposes assessing an existing quote journey. It does **not**
establish an actual client problem. General business relevance, a contact invitation and
152 saved pages do not by themselves prove lost enquiries, poor usability or buyer demand.

## Corrected integration

- Preparation waits for pending/running screenshot and PageSpeed jobs as well as source research.
- Writer input excludes unverified narrative summaries; exact source quotes and a clearly labelled proposed action remain.
- Assessment and writing receive a separate, bounded audit context. Lab numbers are not inserted
  into public HTML quotes. Current same-page reports only; seven-day expiry and latest-crawl
  scope apply. A newer failed audit supersedes an older success.
- A complete render can supply a scoped horizontal-overflow observation. Partial renders,
  zero form counts, unavailable widgets and DOM label heuristics cannot establish missing features.
- Valid Lighthouse scores and numeric metrics reach the writer. For website/store improvement,
  an available qualifying observation must be referenced with its measured value and lab/capture
  qualification. References and audit input are saved with AI provenance; changed/expired audits
  invalidate approval and audit changes during generation preserve the old draft.
- A score below 90 is an observation to investigate, not a conversion-loss calculation. Client
  pain and commercial impact remain unconfirmed. A model can still make semantic mistakes;
  exact numeric/reference checks are narrower than a factual-entailment guarantee.
- PageSpeed queues once after research when AUTO_PERFORMANCE_AUDIT=true and a key is configured.
  Without a key, manual testing remains available. This does not grant provider quota or repair
  HTTP 429 by itself. Google recommends a key for repeated automated requests:
  https://developers.google.com/speed/docs/insights/v5/get-started
- The prospect summary exposes audit coverage, missing measurements and whether the saved draft
  predates structured audit inputs. Existing edited email text is preserved.

## Remaining limits

A complete quote/chatbot/checkout interaction audit, form submission testing, visual reasoning
over screenshots, private analytics, actual conversion loss and client-confirmed priorities are
not established by this implementation. No login/CAPTCHA bypass or invented pain point is used.
PageSpeed quota/configuration and platform capture failures remain explicit provider/source gaps.
The automation should offer an evidence-based experiment or hold weak prospects when there is
no defensible problem; it must not manufacture a problem to make the email sound specific.
