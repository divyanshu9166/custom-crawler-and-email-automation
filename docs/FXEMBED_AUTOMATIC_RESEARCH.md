## Integration

The free community adapter is used in both company-associated professional research and initial X business-signal resolution after a native public capture is unavailable. Initial discovery can retain bio/email hints before a website has been resolved. Explicit website strings in a returned bio become research hints; current official content and reciprocal links still establish candidate matches independently. Mirror data does not overwrite the native observation, verify country/identity/buyer ownership or qualify an email for sending. Unsupported post/search/private routes cannot become profile API lookups. A disabled public failover setting still prevents managed-browser failover.

The dashboard's Find prospects view identifies the enabled community source. Company details display mirror provenance, retrieval/cache state, bio text, observed inbox purpose and pending buyer review. Initial source signals also display their community hint separately. Native and community source states remain distinct; unavailable/missing mirror responses do not erase usable official-site research.
